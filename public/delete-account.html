Delete your DrillRig account

DrillRig — Borewell Billing (app on Google Play: "DrillRig – Borewell Billing")

You can ask us to delete your DrillRig account and all its data at any time. It is free and takes up to 30 days.

How to ask

  1. Send a WhatsApp message to +91 83000 30123 with the words "Delete my DrillRig account" and the Google (Gmail) address you sign in with.
  2. We confirm it is your account (we may ask you to send the message from that Gmail address or from the mobile number saved in the app).
  3. We delete the account and reply when it is done (within 30 days).

Before asking, you can keep a copy of your data: in the app open Settings → Backup and download the file.

What is deleted

  • Your DrillRig sign-in record and the mobile number you saved.
  • If you are the only owner of a business: the business and all its bills, quotations, customers, payments, rates, settings and team invites.
  • If you are staff in someone else's business: your access to it. The bills you made stay in that owner's bill book, because they are the owner's business records.

What is kept

  • Nothing from your business is kept after deletion. Data on your own phone stays there until you clear the app or its site data.
  • Google keeps its own records of your Google account; delete DrillRig's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

See also our Privacy Policy.
