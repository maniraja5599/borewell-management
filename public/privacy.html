Privacy Policy

DrillRig — Borewell Billing & Accounts · Last updated: 15 November 2026

In short: you sign in with Google to use DrillRig. Your bills and customer details are saved on your phone and in your business's cloud account, so they are backed up and your other phones can use them. The DrillRig team can see this data to run and support the service; every time we open it, it is recorded. We do not sell your data or show ads. We count visits with Google Analytics (screens used, no personal details).

1. What DrillRig stores

  • Business data you type in: your company profile (name, phone numbers, address, logo, GSTIN, UPI ID), rates, customers (name, mobile number, village/site), bills, quotations, payments and notes.
  • Your Google account: your name, email address and profile picture, which Google shares with us when you choose "Continue with Google". Signing in is required to use the app.
  • Your mobile number: asked once after you sign in, so the DrillRig team can support you and the owner of your business can see who is in the team. It is not shown to your customers unless you add it to your bills.
  • Team invites: when an owner invites someone by mobile number, we keep that number, the name typed and a one-time invite code until the invite is used, cancelled or expires (7 days).

We do not ask for, and do not store, your Google password, contacts, location, or any other Google data.

2. Where it is stored

  • On your phone: everything is saved in your browser's storage on your device, so the app works offline.
  • In the cloud: your business data is copied to a database run by Supabase, hosted in Mumbai, India. Only the people in your company account can read it in the app. It is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.
  • What the DrillRig team can see: to run the service, give support, handle subscriptions and find problems, the DrillRig team can see everything stored in your business's cloud account: account details (name, email, business name, staff and their roles, rigs, subscription dates), your company profile (phone, address, GSTIN, UPI ID, bill numbering, footer notes) and rate cards, totals (bills, feet drilled, amounts billed, received and due), and your bills and customers (names, mobile numbers, villages, amounts and payments). This access is limited to the DrillRig team, is read-only, and every time we open a business's profile, report, bills or customers it is recorded in our admin log. We never share it with anyone else or contact your customers.

3. How it is used

Your data is used to run the app for you (calculate and save bills, show customer dues, share receipts, keep your phones in sync and backed up) and to support and improve the service (for example, checking a bill you ask us about, or seeing which features are used). We do not use it for advertising and do not sell or rent it to anyone.

4. Services the app uses

  • Google Sign-In — to sign you in (Google Privacy Policy).
  • Supabase — cloud database and sign-in.
  • GitHub Pages — hosts the app's web files.
  • Public code libraries (unpkg.com, cdnjs.cloudflare.com, Google Fonts) — load icons, fonts and the PDF/image export tools. Like any website, these servers can see your IP address when the files are downloaded.
  • Google Analytics — counts visits and which screens are used (for example "Bills", "Rates"), the type of phone and the city-level area. It never receives names, phone numbers, bills or customer details, and advertising features are switched off. (How Google uses this data)
  • WhatsApp — when you tap "Share on WhatsApp", the app opens WhatsApp with the message ready. DrillRig never sends messages by itself.

5. Your choices

  • Sign-in is required: DrillRig needs a Google sign-in so your data is backed up in your account. If you do not want your data in the cloud, please do not use DrillRig.
  • Download your data: Settings → Backup gives you a full copy as a file at any time.
  • Delete your data: delete bills or customers in the app, or clear the app's site data on your phone. To delete your cloud account and all its data, follow the steps on Delete your account and we will delete it within 30 days.
  • Remove Google access: you can disconnect DrillRig at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

6. Customer details you enter

You are responsible for having your customers' permission to store their names and phone numbers and to message them. DrillRig only keeps these details for your business use as described above.

7. Children

DrillRig is a business tool and is not meant for children under 18.

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
