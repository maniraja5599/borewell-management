Privacy Policy

DrillRig — Borewell Billing & Accounts · Last updated: 14 November 2026

In short: your bills and customer details are saved on your own phone. If you choose to sign in with Google, they are also saved in your private cloud account so your other phones can use them. We do not sell your data, show ads, or use tracking or analytics.

1. What DrillRig stores

  • Business data you type in: your company profile (name, phone numbers, address, logo, GSTIN, UPI ID), rates, customers (name, mobile number, village/site), bills, quotations, payments and notes.
  • If you sign in with Google: your Google account name, email address and profile picture, which Google shares with us when you choose "Continue with Google".

We do not ask for, and do not store, your Google password, contacts, location, or any other Google data.

2. Where it is stored

  • On your phone: everything is saved in your browser's storage on your device, so the app works offline.
  • In the cloud (only if you sign in): your business data is copied to a database run by Supabase, hosted in Mumbai, India. Only the people in your company account can read it in the app. It is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.
  • What the DrillRig team can see: to run the service and subscriptions, we can see account details (name, email, business name, staff and their roles, rigs, subscription dates) and counts such as how many bills and customers are stored and when the account was last used. We open your bills and customer details only when you ask us for support, to fix a problem with your account, or when the law requires it. Every such access is recorded in our admin log.

3. How it is used

Your data is used only to run the app for you: to calculate and save bills, show customer dues, share receipts, and keep your phones in sync. We do not use it for advertising and do not sell or rent it to anyone.

4. Services the app uses

  • Google Sign-In — to sign you in (Google Privacy Policy).
  • Supabase — cloud database for signed-in users.
  • GitHub Pages — hosts the app's web files.
  • Public code libraries (unpkg.com, cdnjs.cloudflare.com, Google Fonts) — load icons, fonts and the PDF/image export tools. Like any website, these servers can see your IP address when the files are downloaded.
  • WhatsApp — when you tap "Share on WhatsApp", the app opens WhatsApp with the message ready. DrillRig never sends messages by itself.

5. Your choices

  • Use it without an account: sign-in is optional. Without it, nothing leaves your phone except what you choose to share.
  • Download your data: Settings → Backup gives you a full copy as a file at any time.
  • Delete your data: delete bills or customers in the app, or clear the app's site data on your phone. To delete your cloud account and all its data, contact us (below) from your signed-in email address and we will delete it within 30 days.
  • Remove Google access: you can disconnect DrillRig at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

6. Customer details you enter

You are responsible for having your customers' permission to store their names and phone numbers and to message them. DrillRig only keeps these details for your business use as described above.

7. Children

DrillRig is a business tool and is not meant for children under 18.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
